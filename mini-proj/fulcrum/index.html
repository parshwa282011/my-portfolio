Fulcrum

A block world where what you build obeys real physics.

Controls
  • W A S D move · Space jump · Shift sneak · Ctrl sprint · V fly
  • Left click break · Right click place · 1–9 / wheel choose block
  • F assemble the structure you look at into a physics body
  • R sit at the helm / get off · W S throttle · A D steer · G put the structure back
  • Tab settings and physics · Esc release the mouse

Needs a browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome, Edge or Safari).
